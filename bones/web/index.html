Where these numbers come from. Every status, urgency band, error value and prevention line is produced by the project's own controller — HealthController(std=100, warning=0.05, critical=0.10, warning_rate=0.01, critical_rate=0.02, prediction_days=7) — and by prevention.recommend("bone_density", …). Those are the same thresholds test.py uses to build the Unity bone report. The readings themselves are simulated mission profiles, not measured crew data.
Not a medical device. A reading inside the band does not establish bone health.
