How this differs from the blood monitor. The blood panel compares you with your own pre-flight baseline. This one compares you with a modelled expected value: the published group trajectory scaled to your baseline. Your personal change and the group change are shown side by side.
Reference: Balali et al. (2025), 17 ISS cosmonauts, doi:10.1038/s41526-025-00522-8. The tolerance values are fictional and no individual clinical range is established.
Not a medical device. Readouts support a review conversation and never diagnose.
