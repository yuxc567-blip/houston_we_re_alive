## Editorial decisions

- The risk discussion is explicitly anchored to the 2022 report, not asserted to be current clinical guidance. Possible future irreversible loss is distinguished from outcomes observed as of that report.
- Use Laurie’s primary precision methods to explain the variability band; avoid presenting it as a probability of individual disease or a population-mean CI.
- The OCT sequence is a study-method summary, not an operational procedure. Do not invent positioning, instrument button presses, calibration, scan acceptance limits or clinical escalation deadlines.
- The record/quality/medical-review checklist is labeled as the prototype workflow. No treatment or medication is recommended by the numerical flag.
- The anatomy schematic is original, simplified and not to scale. It does not depict patient data or diagnose pathology.

## Crew-interface revision

The official Space Apps challenge search result was retrieved on 2026-11-15 and states that the software should gather health indicators and enable astronauts to evaluate and act on health status. Direct retrieval of the detailed challenge page still returned 502; no unseen requirements are attributed to it. Overview now prioritizes simultaneous per-eye findings, manual entry, concise next steps and on-demand input help. The trend chart is on History; descriptive metrics and long educational content are collapsed. Color indicates the existing research finding or data availability, never a newly invented medical severity score.
