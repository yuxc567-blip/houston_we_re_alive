What do rate and accumulated deviation mean?

Rate compares readings over their actual mission-day interval. The first reading has no rate. Accumulated deviation is the signed trapezoidal integral from the first recorded observation, with linear interpolation between readings. Positive and negative values can cancel. It is not cumulative sleep debt or a validated damage score.

The journal shows descriptive comparisons only. It does not assign medical urgency or explain the cause of a change.
