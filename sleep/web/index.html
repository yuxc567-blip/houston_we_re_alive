What the numbers mean. Both analyses come from the module's own code, unchanged: sleep/sleep_duration.py and sleep/reactiontime.py. Positive deviation means less sleep than the reference or slower than your baseline. The cumulative column integrates the deviation over recorded days, with above-reference days offsetting below-reference ones. It is not total sleep debt.
Bands. Steady under 5%, notable to 15%, marked beyond. Those cut-offs are a presentation device for this page; neither module defines a clinical threshold, and a value outside them is not a finding.
Sleep reference: eight hours per 24 hours, the adult consensus cited by the module (AASM). Reaction baseline is a fictional personal value. Readings on this page are invented. Not a medical device.
