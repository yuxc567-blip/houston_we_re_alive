YOUR NEXT STEP
What does the +19.4 μm flag mean?

An increase of more than 19.4 μm from your own preflight baseline is the research signal for early optic disc edema (ODE): swelling at the optic nerve head. It is not a vision-loss threshold or a diagnosis of SANS.

Persistent swelling is a concern for nerve-fibre damage over longer missions. A flag does not mean that damage has occurred. Evidence and limits →

New visual changes? Follow your mission's medical reporting procedure even if TRT is below threshold.
